# Workload Cybersecurity Risk Prioritization Action Planning

**Pack:** NIST CSF Pack  
**Description:** Workload architectures should incorporate a structured approach to identifying, assessing, and prioritizing cybersecurity risks in alignment with the workload's mission, risk appetite, and available resources. This includes conducting a gap analysis between the current security posture and the desired target state, and translating identified gaps into a prioritized action plan (such as a risk register, risk detail report, or Plan of Action and Milestones). Risk responses — whether mitigating, transferring, avoiding, or accepting — should be chosen based on assessed threats, vulnerabilities, likelihoods, and impacts, and tracked over time to ensure continuity of mission-essential functions.

## Domain / Applicability

Applies during workload design, architecture review, and ongoing security posture management — particularly when defining security requirements, evaluating trade-offs, or planning iterative security improvements.

## Evaluation Criteria

COMPLIANT: The design includes or references a risk prioritization process that identifies security gaps relative to a target posture, documents risk responses with rationale (mitigate, transfer, avoid, or accept), and maintains a prioritized action plan (e.g., risk register, POA&M, or equivalent backlog) aligned to workload mission and resource constraints. OR the design provides documented justification for an alternative risk management approach that achieves equivalent visibility and traceability of security decisions.

NON-COMPLIANT: The design lacks any evidence of risk gap analysis, prioritized security action planning, or documented risk response decisions — leaving security trade-offs untracked and unaligned to workload mission or risk tolerance.
